Questions

Is there a free trial?

No. The free plan is the trial: use it for as long as you like, with no card.

What happens when we cancel?

Nothing is deleted. If your library is over the free limits, you can still open and download everything, but not add more until you are back under them.

How do we ask for a refund?

Email support@scriptural.online within 14 days of paying for a year.

Do we need a plan on every computer?

No. One plan covers your church. People are invited to the church library, and the app is free on each computer.

Does it work without the internet?

Songs, public-domain Bibles and service plans stay on your computer. Scriptural AI and syncing the church library need the internet.
