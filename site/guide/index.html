Get started

  1. Download the app for your computer from the home page and open it.
  2. Set up your screens. The app puts the words on the projector or TV, and keeps the operator's page on your own screen.
  3. Add your songs. Type them in, or bring in a library (see Songs).
  4. Try a verse. Say or type “John 3:16” and watch it come up.

Everything works without an account. An account is only for the shared church library.

Songs

Songs are kept as sections, such as Verse 1 and Chorus, and the order they are sung. A chorus sung three times is stored once.

  • Bring a library in from EasyWorship, ProPresenter and others, using the import options in the app.
  • Type a song with a title and its words. Put a label like Chorus on its own line to start a section.
  • Show a song by clicking its section, or tell Matthias “Back to the chorus”.

Scripture

Every public-domain Bible is included. Licensed versions such as the NIV and NKJV are available on request with a plan.

  • Listen: the Live Copilot hears the preacher and brings up the verse as it is read. It can show the verse by itself, or wait for your click.
  • Ask: “John three sixteen”, “Read that in the NLT”, or “Find the verse about being anxious”.
  • Move on: “Next verse” steps the screen while the preacher keeps going.

Speech is turned into text on your own computer.

Service plans

A plan is the order of your meeting: songs, scripture, pictures, videos and notes. Build it ahead of time, then run it with one button, or say “Start the service” and “Next item”.

  • Add anything you can show to a plan, and drag to reorder.
  • Taking an item off a plan never deletes the song or file. If you remove one by mistake, Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on a Mac) brings it back.

Matthias, your assistant

Matthias does what you ask, by voice or by typing, and can do several things one after another. Its panel sits beside the app, so you can keep working while it helps.

Let it lead

Turn on Agent mode in Matthias settings and it runs the screen by itself. Move anything by hand and it waits for you. Say “Take over” to hold the screen, “Stand down” to hand it back. Locking the screen stops everything.

Draw and make lists

Ask Matthias to illustrate what the preacher is saying: “Draw what the preacher is describing” or “Draw a cross”. It draws simple shapes over the screen. Say “Clean up the drawing” to tidy it, or “Clear the drawing” to remove it. You can also draw by hand on the Draw page and press Clean up.

Say “List the main points” and Matthias saves a list slide from what was said. It goes on screen only when you ask. The Draw page has buttons for all of this.

Screens, timer and remote

  • Backgrounds: hundreds made for churches, with loops and worship pads. Download the video loops to keep your own copy.
  • Speaker timer: a countdown only the speaker sees.
  • Stage screen: what is up, what is next, and the time, for the platform.
  • Phone remote: move the slides from anywhere in the room.
  • Clear and restore: blank the screen and bring it back, with a button or by voice.

Church library

The church library keeps your church's songs in one place and copies them to every computer, so the sound desk and the front match. Songs stay usable offline once copied.

  1. In the app, open Import, then Church library.
  2. Sign in with your email. We send a code. There is no password.
  3. Type your church's name to start it, or enter an invite code to join one.
  4. Use Invite someone to bring in your worship team. The code works for 14 days.
  5. That is all. Songs sync by themselves while the computer is online. Sync now does it straight away.

The free plan covers 3 people and 500 songs. See plans.

Other AI assistants

If you use Claude Code or another AI agent, it can run the app too: find scripture, build plans, show a song, start the timer. In the app, open Settings, then Connection, then AI agents, turn it on and copy the command. It answers only on your own computer and needs the token you copied. It never deletes a song, plan or file.
