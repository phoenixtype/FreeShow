Video loops for your church

Scriptural has 183 motion loops, countdowns and gradients for worship, seasons and the cross, all free for churches to use. Most are made by us. Some are public-domain film from nature and space, cut so they repeat without a jump.

Use them in the app

Nothing to download. In the app, open Media, then Online, then Scriptural, pick a category, and click a loop. New loops appear there without an update.

Add your own

Put your own loops in a folder and add that folder under Media in the app. It plays MP4, MOV and WebM. For a smooth loop, use 1920×1080 at 30 frames a second, 15 to 30 seconds long, with the last second fading into the first.
