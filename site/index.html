Songs and scripture on screen, for church meetings

Scriptural Project runs the screens in the room. It listens to the preacher and brings up each verse as it is read, so no one has to hunt for it.

All downloads

Hears the verse

Say "John 3:16", quote it, or tell the story of the prodigal son. The verse comes up on screen, or waits for a click if you prefer.

Follows the reading

"Next verse", "go to chapter four" and "last verse" move the screen while the preacher keeps going.

Runs on your computer

Speech is turned into text on the computer itself. Songs, Bibles and media stay on it too.

Free, with plans for your team

The app is free. A plan adds a shared church library, Scriptural AI and licensed Bible versions. See plans.
